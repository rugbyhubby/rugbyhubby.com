Guess The Word Dummy — a phone-on-forehead party game

How to Play

Choose a category, then put the phone on your forehead. Your friends give you clues to the word on the screen without saying the word itself. Say the word out loud, or tilt the phone down when you get it right. If you can't figure it out, tilt the phone up to skip. You have 60 seconds per round.

Frequently Asked Questions

Tilt detection isn't registering my gestures correctly.

Make sure you're holding the phone steady for a moment when the round starts, before the countdown finishes — this lets the app calibrate to how you're holding it. If tilting still feels off after that, try a firmer, more deliberate tilt rather than a quick flick.

The app didn't hear me say the correct word.

Voice detection works best in a quieter room. It listens continuously during your turn, so if it misses what you said, you can still just tilt the phone as a fallback.

Do I need an internet connection to play?

Yes — categories and words are loaded from the app's servers, so an internet connection is required.
