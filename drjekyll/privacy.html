Dr. Jekyll

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

The short version Dr. Jekyll does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal health information. Ever. Your data stays on your device.

What Dr. Jekyll Does

Dr. Jekyll is a personal lab result monitoring app for cancer patients. It reads lab results from Apple Health on your iPhone, displays them as trend charts over time, and helps you prepare better questions for your care team.

What We Collect

Nothing.

Dr. Jekyll does not have a backend server, a database, an account system, analytics, crash reporting, or any network connection of its own. There is no company server that your data touches.

The only information Dr. Jekyll stores on your device is your app preferences — which lab tests you have chosen to monitor and your date range settings. This is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework and never leaves your device.

Your Health Data

Dr. Jekyll reads lab results from Apple Health using Apple's HealthKit framework. This requires your explicit permission, which you grant through Apple's standard health authorization prompt.

  • Dr. Jekyll reads specific lab result types that you choose to monitor.
  • Dr. Jekyll never writes to Apple Health.
  • Dr. Jekyll never stores your lab values on disk. Results are loaded into memory when you view them and discarded when you leave the screen.
  • Dr. Jekyll never transmits your health data anywhere. No lab values, no dates, no identifiers leave your device through Dr. Jekyll.

You can revoke Dr. Jekyll's access to Apple Health at any time:

Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Dr. Jekyll

Ask Claude Feature

Dr. Jekyll includes a feature that helps you build a prompt to bring into a conversation with Claude AI (made by Anthropic). This feature:

  • Builds a text prompt on your device using your selected lab results
  • Shows you the full prompt before anything happens
  • Copies the prompt to your clipboard when you choose to proceed
  • Opens the Claude app or website, where you paste the prompt yourself

Dr. Jekyll itself never sends any data to Claude or Anthropic. The prompt only leaves your device when you choose to paste and send it in the Claude app. You are in full control of what you share.

Face ID

Dr. Jekyll uses Face ID (or your device passcode as a fallback) to prevent others from viewing your health data if they pick up your phone. Face ID authentication is handled entirely by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. Dr. Jekyll never sees or stores your biometric data.

Demo Mode

Dr. Jekyll includes a Demo Mode that displays sample lab data for a fictional Multiple Myeloma patient. Demo Mode does not use or display any of your real health data. It is designed to let you explore the app before connecting your health records.

Children's Privacy

Dr. Jekyll is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page. Since Dr. Jekyll collects no data, changes are expected to be minimal.
